## A. 30-second pitch

> AI agents need to pay for data and compute, but giving them a wallet makes one
> bad instruction expensive. Allowance puts enforceable spending boundaries on
> Portaldot V3. Owners set budgets, caps, recipients and expiry. Agents pay
> autonomously within those rules; out-of-policy payments wait for owner approval,
> while frozen or expired access is blocked. The owner keeps control without
> signing every routine payment.

## B. Two-minute demo script

Use a fresh browser session with no wallet connected. Serve the current branch's
`docs/` directory. All amounts below are from the actual wallet-free simulation,
not the deployed testnet account. Wait for the outcome panel after every action;
a preview or submitted transaction is not a completed payment.

| Time | Action | Say / point out |
|---|---|---|
| 0–12s | Open landing; show headline and Policy Field. | “The agent is the node, the boundary is the owner's on-chain spending policy, and the route shows the decision. Distance is not a financial limit.” |
| 12–22s | Select **Owner approval**, then **Policy block**, then **Autonomous payment**. | “These are labeled illustrations. A cap violation would create a request; a frozen agent cannot pay or create one.” |
| 22–35s | Click **Try Interactive Demo**. Wait for simulation readiness. | “This runs the real contract bytecode in this browser. No wallet, real tokens or testnet transactions are involved.” |
| 35–48s | Show Agent Control Field and its explicit readings. | “The cap is 1 POT per payment and 5 POT per day. 4 POT remains in the autonomous allowance; 19 POT is held by the contract. Those are different quantities. Two seeded requests await a decision.” |
| 48–60s | In agent tester, keep the default trusted recipient and amount **0.1**; click **Send as agent**. | After confirmation: “Paid autonomously.” Contract balance is **18.9 POT**; autonomous allowance is **3.9 POT**. |
| 60–75s | Enter **2.5**; show the approval preview, then submit. | “A preview records nothing. Now the confirmed request awaits owner approval. Contract balance and allowance did not change; funds are not reserved.” |
| 75–92s | Approve the newly created request in the inbox (request #2 in a fresh run). | After confirmation: “Paid after owner approval.” Balance is **16.4 POT**, while autonomous allowance remains **3.9 POT**. The two original demo requests remain pending. |
| 92–108s | Change the next amount back to **0.1**. Click **Freeze agent**. | Point to the red field, frozen status, actual blocked preview and disabled payment button. “No payment or new approval request can proceed.” |
| 108–120s | Click **Unfreeze** and wait for active status/preview. | “Routine autonomy returns under exactly the same rules. The owner controls the boundary.” |

If any action is still confirming, wait rather than narrating a success. Do not
click Deploy or connect a real wallet during this rehearsal. If the presenter
needs a restart, use a new browser context/session and launch the simulation again;
its state is in memory and the seed is repeatable. Review screenshots are a
fallback visual aid, not evidence of a newly executed transaction.

